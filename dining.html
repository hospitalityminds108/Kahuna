Three Kitchens. One Philosophy.

Fresh, coastal, unhurried. Full meals at Coastal Cravings, all-day bites at Flamingo Lounge and tandoor evenings on the Breezy Bites rooftop.

The Main Restaurant

Coastal Cravings

Breakfast, lunch and dinner, done properly. Buffet or à la carte — Konkan classics like fish curry with solkadi and Malvani specialities, alongside tandoor, biryanis, continental and a generous vegetarian spread. Seats 80–90: easy for a quiet dinner for two, or a whole team eating together.

  • ●Breakfast, lunch & dinner — buffet and à la carte
  • ●Konkan coastal, tandoor, biryanis & continental
  • ●Seats 80–90 · Full-board and group dining available

All-Day Bites

Flamingo Lounge

For the hours between meals. Grab quick bites, snacks and drinks from 9 AM to 9:30 PM — poolside, between sessions, or whenever the mid-afternoon hunger hits.

  • ●Open 9 AM – 9:30 PM
  • ●Grab-and-go, all-day bites
  • ●Seats approx. 80

Rooftop Tandoor

Breezy Bites

Open-air, rooftop, evenings only. Tandoor specialities and à la carte plates from 7 to 10:30 PM, under the sky. Menus can be curated for small private groups.

The last plate of the day tastes better here, as the sky finishes what the sunset started.

  • ●Open 7:00 PM – 10:30 PM
  • ●Tandoor & à la carte, open-air rooftop
  • ●Seats 20–25 · Menu curated on request
