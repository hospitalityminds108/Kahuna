The Room · Sea Facing

Made for slow days

The room the resort was built around — a wide window that frames the Arabian Sea like art.

Twin beds in crisp whites against a teal headboard, warm wood cabinetry, an en-suite bath and quiet, considered lighting. Mornings begin with tea facing the water. Nights end with the sound of it.

Your day, within a minute's walk: the shore at fifty metres, the infinity pool on the way, and the lounge for after.
