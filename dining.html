Home /Dining

Two kitchens. One philosophy.

Fresh, coastal and unhurried — from the day's catch at Coastal Crave to slow coffee on the rooftop at Breezy Bites.

The Main Restaurant

Coastal Crave

The kitchen the coast wrote. Konkan seafood and comfort classics, cooked from the day's catch and served without hurry — fish curries with solkadhi, ghee-roasted everything, thali plates, and continental favourites for the less adventurous.

Long breakfasts before the beach, lazy lunches by the pool and dinners that stretch into conversation.

  • ●Konkan coastal cuisine & continental classics
  • ●Fresh catch, local produce, seasonal menus
  • ●Breakfast, lunch & dinner for residents & guests

The Rooftop Cafe

Breezy Bites

Upstairs, where the wind is — the rooftop pours coffee, shakes, juices and all-day bites against the horizon. Blue tables, golden light and the small hunger in between meals.

The last coffee of the day is best taken here, as the sun goes to work on the sky.

  • ●Coffee, shakes, juices & smoothies
  • ●All-day bites, snacks & short eats
  • ●Sunset-facing rooftop seating
