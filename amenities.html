The Resort

Everything Within a Short Walk

Kahuna is a beach resort in Vengurla, 50 minutes from Goa’s Mopa Airport. Swim in the infinity pool, walk to the sand, rally on the pickleball court, eat at three venues and end the day by the water — nothing is more than a few minutes apart.

The Swimming Pool

An Infinity Pool That Meets the Sea

The pool is the heart of Kahuna. A long stretch of blue with the Arabian Sea behind it, loungers along the deck, lawns and palms on either side — and a sunset that lands right on the water.

  • Infinity edge facing the beach and the sunset
  • Sun loungers, shade and lawns around the deck
  • A short walk from every room and all three dining venues
  • Poolside bites from Flamingo Lounge, 9 AM – 9:30 PM

Beach & Water Sports

Fifty Metres to the Sand

A quiet, uncrowded stretch of the Konkan coast — with water sports when the sea’s in the mood.

The Quiet Beach

Uncrowded sand for slow morning walks and long sunsets — the calm side of the coast, a short drive from Goa.

Jet Skis

Snorkelling

Dining

Three Places to Eat

Fresh, coastal, unhurried — all a few steps from your room.

Coastal Cravings

Breakfast, lunch and dinner — buffet or à la carte. Konkan classics, tandoor, biryanis and continental. Seats 80–90.

Flamingo Lounge

All-day bites, snacks and drinks from 9 AM to 9:30 PM — poolside, or between sessions.

Breezy Bites

Open-air rooftop tandoor from 7 to 10:30 PM. Menus can be curated for small private groups.

Offsites & Team Training

Learning Bays for Teams

Two classroom-style Learning Bays seat around 80, run on 24/7 Wi-Fi and open onto the pool and the beach. Corporate teams and university groups stay, eat and work in one place.

  • 2 Learning Bays · around 80 seats
  • Stays for up to 90 guests (100 at most)
  • Full-board dining and quick-bite breaks
  • Airport shuttle from Goa (Mopa) and Sindhudurg
