Our Story

Built on a quiet shore of the Konkan

On a calm stretch of coast at Ubhandanda, Vengurla — far enough from the noise, close enough to the sea — Kahuna was built around a simple idea: that rest and growth are not opposites. That the best thinking happens where the pace slows.

By day, it's a sanctuary for learners and teams — classrooms with a sea breeze, breakouts by the pool. By evening, it's a quiet beachfront escape where sunsets close the laptop and open the evening.

We look forward to welcoming you to a space where the pace slows, ideas flow, and every stay feels intentional.

Where We Are

Vengurla, Sindhudurg — the quiet Konkan

Kahuna sits on a calm beach stretch at Ubhandanda, Vengurla — in Sindhudurg, Maharashtra's southernmost coastal district. Uncrowded sands, working ports, lighthouse sunsets and some of the cleanest water on the west coast.

  • ●50 min from Manohar International Airport (Goa)
  • ●40 min from Sindhudurg Airport
  • ●Minutes from Vengurla's beaches, port and lighthouse
