Good to Know

Questions, answered

Kahuna sits on a quiet beach stretch at Ubhandanda, Vengurla, in Sindhudurg, Maharashtra — roughly 50 minutes from Manohar International Airport (Goa) and 40 minutes from Sindhudurg Airport.

Five categories: Sea View Rooms, Fountain View Rooms, Garden View Rooms, Fountain View Suites and Sea View Suites — all with king or twin bedding, en-suite baths, AC and tea/coffee.

Yes — Kahuna doubles as a learning sanctuary with classroom-style Learning Bays, meeting spaces, sea-facing calm and full board dining, purpose-built for engineering-college programs, bootcamps and team offsites.

Coastal Crave, our main restaurant serving Konkan coastal cuisine and continental favourites, and Breezy Bites, the rooftop cafe for coffees, shakes and all-day bites.

An infinity pool with loungers, a pickleball court, a residents' lounge, water sports at the beach, high-speed Wi-Fi across public areas and Learning Bays, and parking.
