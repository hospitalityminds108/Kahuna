Why Here

The Classrooms

Two Bays, One
Sea Breeze

The Learning Bays are classroom-style rooms with tall windows, natural light, air conditioning and seating for around 80 in total. Whiteboards, projectors and flexible layouts — rows for sessions, clusters for breakouts, open space for builds.

Between bays: the lounge for slow conversations, the pool for lunch-hour resets, and the sea for the problem you can’t solve at a desk.

  • Air-conditioned, with natural light
  • Projectors, whiteboards and flexible seating
  • 24/7 high-speed Wi-Fi
